<p>Collect 2 horses from paddock, put one in wait yard, one in test

<p><em>Eye temp:</em></p>
<p>Lead to IR camera position, lead through poles and stop.</p>
<p>Take eye temp with FLIR 1m away (use mark on ground), at 90 degree
angle to horse.</p>
<p>Check image is in focus and captures eye. If yes, label and save with
session type and horse name if not, retake shot</p>
<p><em>Saliva:</em></p>
<p>Collect saliva with forceps and sponge, hold in mouth for approx.
1min, avoid horse chewing on sponge.</p>
<p>Label tube with time, horse name and session type, date</p>
<p>Put in esky, put forceps in forceps water bowl, swish around then put
forceps to dry in tube rack until next sampling</p>
<p>Turn on HRM, check working then turn off.</p>
<p>Commence session habituation session:</p>
<p>Lead horse around CB arena and allow the horse to explore.</p>
<p>Afterwards, lead horse up to the robot and allow to explore/
sniff/touch the device.</p>
<p>When they are showing showing signs of losing interest, handfuls of
feed will be dropped into the bowl and the horses allowed to consume
it.</p>
<p>After 3-5 repetitions of manual dropping of feed, the servo motor
will be used to dispense the feed via the feeder tube.</p>
<p>This will be repeated until the horse no longer reacts to the sound
of the servo motor action or the feed dispensing and remains stationary
during servo operation and feed dispense.</p></td>

<td>6-7</td>
<td>RP-A 1- acquisition session,</td>
<td>20 trials</td>
<td><p>Criterion- panel touch within 10 s of hearing the start tone.</p>
<p>80% of responses within 10 s</p></td>
<td><p>Eye temp</p>
<p>Collect saliva</p>
<p>Start HRM-note time</p>
<p>For the first three trials, a small amount of feed will be placed on
the bottom shelf of the Perspex barrier that surrounds the touch panel
prior the playing of the start tone to encourage the horse to poke its
nose into the square and so make contact with the touch panel.</p>
<p>Horse is led into start race (2 jump poles) and up to start pole
placed 2.5m from the device and held there until the start tone
plays.</p>
<p>Horse then led over the pole and left to explore the device.
Consuming the feed on the shelf will trigger sensor, or the sensor will
be triggered manually if necessary, and then the correct response tone
will play and the feed will be dispensed.</p>
<p>At the conclusion of the feed consumption the horse will be led back
over to the start pole and the sequence will be repeated. Data logged by
the device.</p>
<p>20 trials per session.</p>
<p>After the first three shaping trials, no feed will be placed on the
shelf around the touch panel and the sensor will only be triggered,
either automatically or manually when the horse makes contact with the
panel with its nose.</p>
<p>Once the horse is responding to the start tone without having to be
led up to the device, the handler will remain at a distance to the horse
during each trial only interacting with the horse to lead it back to the
start pole.</p>
<p>Eye temp</p>
<p>Collect saliva</p>
<p>End HRM, note time</p></td>
